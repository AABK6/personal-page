# Product Guidelines

## Prose Style
- **Versatile Tone:** The writing style is highly adaptable. Depending on the specific page or topic, it can range from **analytic and academic** (for data-driven articles) to **narrative and immersive** (for storytelling experiences).

## Visual Branding & Aesthetics
- **Per-Page Identity:** There is no single rigid visual brand. Instead, the visual design and aesthetics should be customized for each page or cluster to best suit its specific topic and mood.

## UX Principles
- **Exploratory Navigation:** Encourage users to interact, click, and discover hidden visual elements or deeper data.
- **Linear Storytelling:** Provide guided, step-by-step progressions for narrative-heavy content.
- **Interactive Data:** Allow users to manipulate variables and observe real-time updates in charts or network graphs.

## Accessibility & Performance
- **Balanced Approach:** Maintain a good baseline of accessibility (e.g., semantic HTML, readable contrast) while ensuring that performance remains a priority to support rich, interactive visual experiences.
