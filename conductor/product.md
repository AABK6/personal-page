# Product Guide

## Vision
To create a highly versatile web platform that tells compelling interactive stories and shares knowledge with the general public. The site serves as both an immersive storytelling medium and a personal test-bed for experimental web development.

## Target Audience
- **General Public:** Individuals interested in engaging narratives, data visualizations, and interactive articles.

## Core Goals
1. **Storytelling & Knowledge Sharing:** Guide users through rich, content-driven experiences that educate and entertain.
2. **Experimentation (Test-Bed):** Maintain a flexible architecture that allows for the integration of new technologies, visualizations, and interaction patterns.

## Key Features
- **Interactive Visuals:** Utilizing canvas-based animations, data charts, and network graphs to bring concepts to life.
- **Content & Articles:** Deep-dive textual content seamlessly integrated with visual elements.

## Tone & Vibe
- **Versatile by Design:** The aesthetic and atmosphere will adapt to suit the specific topic of each page, ranging from cinematic and immersive to clean and minimal as needed.
